Ahlein a warm Lebanese hello

Live on real WhatsApp

“A receptionist that lives on your WhatsApp number.” Customers text or send a voice note, and Ahlein books, moves and cancels real appointments in their own language. The owner watches it land in a companion phone app: the day as columns, one per person, with drag to move and tap to book.

The model translates, the code decides. The LLM turns a sentence into a tool call, and plain Python resolves names, checks availability against people, rooms and chairs, and writes the booking under a database lock. It hears voice notes and reads photos natively, sends approved WhatsApp templates for reminders, and lets staff run the day from the same number. Deployed, and proven end to end on real phones.

Ahlelak POS a till for the corner shop

Desktop app · live web demo

“The shop keeps selling when the internet doesn't.” A point of sale for Lebanese mini-markets: scan a barcode and it's in the cart, priced in dollars and lira at once. Returns, discounts, shifts, a cash count, stock, and the day's numbers, in English and Arabic.

Offline first, by rule. Nothing on the sell path waits on the network. Money is integer minor units, never a float, and stock is a ledger of movements rather than a number someone edits. The same React code ships as a Tauri desktop app for Windows and macOS and runs in the browser on SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, which is the demo you can try.

AI invoice reading then checking the pile

Live demo · try your own invoice

“Reading the invoice is the easy half.” Drop in a month of invoices, receipts and delivery notes, or just email them. Each one is read into clean rows, sorted into money in and money out, and then checked against every document before it.

That second half is the point. No single page shows a supplier's changed bank account, an invoice sent twice, or a price that crept up 23% since March, but the pile does. The checks are plain, tested functions, not a model's opinion, and the model never adds up money: it only reads, and the code does the maths.

  • Claude (vision + tools)
  • Next.js

✦ Nujoom your cosmic decision guide

Najma “star”, in Arabic

“Astrology that answers the question you actually asked.” Its core, Nujoom (Arabic for stars), is an agentic AI decision engine: ask it “best day to ask for a raise?” and it reads your birth chart, scores the timing out of 100, and explains why.

Grounded, not guessed. A deterministic scoring engine computes the numbers on Swiss Ephemeris chart math, and the LLM only narrates them (server-side validation stops it inventing anything). iOS is in App Store review, and the web app is live now.

  • Solo build
  • Agentic AI
  • React Native
  • Swiss Ephemeris
  • Groq LLMs

Senior ML Engineer · 148 CVs Scoring…

hiresort

“Rank applicants before you read every résumé by hand.” An AI screening tool that scores and ranks candidates against the criteria you define, then shows its reasoning (each candidate's strengths and gaps) instead of an opaque match %.

Under the hood: local embeddings pre-filter candidates so only the best consume expensive LLM calls, and scoring runs as chunked, resumable jobs that survive serverless timeouts.
